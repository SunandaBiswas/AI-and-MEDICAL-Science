### 1. Benchmarking Class Imbalance Mitigation Strategies Across Deep CNN Architectures for Skin Cancer Classification

- **Field:** Medical Imaging - Dermatology/Skin Cancer
- **Authors / Poster:** Dr. Irshad Ahmad, Muhammad Khubaib, Saleh Altowaijri
- **Venue:** Diagnostics (MDPI), peer-reviewed
- **Dataset Used:** ISIC 2019
- **Link / DOI:** https://lnkd.in/dJggGe_p
- **Publication Date:** Not specified (recent 2026 issue)
- **LinkedIn Post Recency (as of Sept 16, 2026):** 22h
- **Source Type:** Individual (author, direct share)

Benchmarked 16 class-imbalance mitigation methods across 6 pretrained CNN architectures (EfficientNet-B0/B3, ResNet50, DenseNet121, InceptionV3, MobileNetV2) for skin lesion classification. Best result: Balanced MixUp + EfficientNet-B3, 92.39% accuracy / 92.33% F1. This is the reference post the user linked.
